Our Story

From Workshop to
Global Supplier

Founded in Dongguan — China's manufacturing heartland — JINYUE began as a small workshop producing basic steel piercing jewelry. Over 12+ years, we've grown into a 2,000m² facility with a complete in-house production line, serving clients in 50+ countries.

Our commitment to material safety led us to become an early adopter of G23 ASTM F136 solid titanium — the gold standard for implant-grade body jewelry. Every batch is SGS-tested, and our designs are protected by patents.

Today, we offer 500+ designs across huggie hoops, clicker rings, septum jewelry, heart pavé, and basic essentials — with full OEM/ODM capabilities for custom orders.

What Sets JINYUE Apart

Six pillars that define our commitment to quality and service.

Medical‑Grade Materials

G23 solid titanium (ASTM F136) and 316L surgical steel. Nickel-free, hypoallergenic, safe for healing piercings. Every material batch comes with a test report.

In‑House Production

Cutting, welding, stone setting, polishing, and QC — all under one roof. Vertical integration ensures consistent quality and competitive pricing.

Design Patents

Original designs protected by Chinese design patents. We invest in R&D so our clients can sell distinctive products with confidence.

Flexible OEM / ODM

Custom sizes, gauges, colors, stone types, engravings, and packaging. Low MOQ, fast 7‑day sampling, dedicated project management.

SGS Certified

Third-party SGS testing verifies material composition. Our QC team inspects every piece before packaging — 99.5% pass rate.

Global Shipping

We ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx, UPS, and TNT. Most orders dispatch within 3‑5 days. Express delivery to 50+ countries.

Manufacturing Process

From Raw Metal to Finished Jewelry

01

Precision Cutting

02
