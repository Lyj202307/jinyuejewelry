Manufacturing Process

Crafted Step by Step

Every piece passes through a rigorous multi-stage process — from raw titanium to mirror-polished jewelry.

STEP 01

Precision Cutting

Raw G23 titanium and 316L steel bars are precision-cut to exact dimensions.

STEP 02

Laser Welding

Hinge mechanisms and joints are laser-welded for seamless strength.

STEP 03

Stone Setting

AAA cubic zirconia stones are hand-set using SMT and pave techniques.

STEP 04

Mirror Polishing

Multi-stage polishing achieves a flawless mirror finish, then QC inspected.
